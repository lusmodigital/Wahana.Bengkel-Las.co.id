# Topical Authority — wahana.bengkel-las.co.id

## Role and boundary

This domain is a specialist knowledge and commercial-support property for Indonesian buyers and operators of custom amusement rides. It should help readers plan, specify, select, receive, operate, inspect, maintain, and repair fabricated rides while keeping quotation, consultation, custom-build, delivery, and repair transactions on the existing commercial routes. It does not become a location-page library, a generic tourism site, or a source of unsupported engineering or safety certification claims.

## Context used

- Homepage visible context: the primary entity is **Wahana Permainan**, with custom design, product consultation, delivery, and repair as supporting services.
- Homepage/navigation product labels include komedi putar, bom-bom car, kincir ria, waterboom slide, sepeda air, perahu handboat, istana balon, mandi bola, kereta mini and odong-odong, mainan koin, mainan pegas, mobil wisata, and mobil/becak goes.
- The tracked static export contains 10,009 HTML route files: 8,333 at the root and 1,676 nested.
- Meaningful route families include the homepage, 11 product/company section hubs, and 18 product-category hubs. Representative hubs are `/permainan`, `/air`, `/kereta`, `/kiddi-ride`, `/istana-balon`, `/mainan-koin`, `/mainan-pegas`, `/mandi-bola`, and `/mobil-wisata`.
- There is no tracked generated sitemap XML. The compact route inventory and the repository's sitemap-generation workflow were therefore used as sitemap-equivalent route evidence.
- Safety relevance is a deep-mode signal, but it does not materially change the parent taxonomy. This lean plan avoids invented limits, standards clauses, and certification claims; safety articles require primary-source and qualified-expert review before publication.

## Ignored template noise

- 8,331 root-level `pabrikasi-<produk>-<lokasi>.html` pages are location-swapped templates, not distinct editorial coverage. They span 17 product families, generally 489–491 variants each.
- 816 category-pagination pages and 829 `/berita/page/<n>/` pages are archive/pagination noise.
- Author, feed, comments, asset, WordPress runtime, and legacy `hello-world` paths were excluded.
- Repeated metadata, bodies, media, and regional substitutions were not inspected because they cannot change this taxonomy.

## Internal-link rule

Setiap artikel terhubung ke hub topiknya dan ke satu artikel keputusan atau tindakan berikutnya. Artikel produk menaut ke WAH-03 untuk penyusunan spesifikasi, WAH-07 untuk inspeksi, dan WAH-08 untuk perawatan bila relevan. Artikel diagnosis menaut ke pencegahan serta batas eskalasi, sementara rute konsultasi, custom build, pengiriman, dan repair hanya menerima tautan dari konteks komersial yang benar-benar membantu pembaca.
